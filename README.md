 🛒 Nexa Retail Dashboard — Electronics Analytics

 📌 Project Overview
A comprehensive analytics dashboard built for an electronics retail company called Nexa Retail.
It covers four key areas: Sales, Customers, Inventory, and Returns.
The goal is to transform raw data into actionable business decisions.

---

 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used

 Power BI
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation (ETL)
- **Data Modeling** — Building relationships between tables
- **DAX** — Writing custom measures and calculations

 1️⃣ Sales


<img width="891" height="546" alt="1000097662" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6678e66b-7fb2-4e34-add7-33c449b43d39" />




Key Numbers:
- 💰 Total Revenue: $7.6M
- 📈 Profit Margin: 69%
- 🛍️ Total Orders: 2453

🔍 Insight:
A clear drop in sales was noticed from July to October,
indicating the business heavily relies on end-of-year seasonality.

✅ Recommendation:
Launch targeted marketing campaigns during slow months
to maintain consistent revenue throughout the year.

2️⃣ Customer

<img width="901" height="544" alt="1000097663" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/220666b5-e273-4efe-894b-41671073e42d" />


Key Numbers:
- 👥 Total Customers: 1,119
- 🆕 New Customers: 152
- 💵 Avg Order Value: $3,100

🔍 Insight:
19% of customers are Inactive — meaning 208 customers
who previously purchased have stopped buying entirely.

✅ Recommendation:
Launch a targeted Retention campaign with special offers
for Inactive customers, since re-engaging an existing customer
costs significantly less than acquiring a new one.

 3️⃣ Product & Inventory 

<img width="905" height="539" alt="1000097664" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/82d5ff22-4ebf-4789-a7cd-7b9bcf258efc" />


Key Numbers:
- 📦 Stock Value: $32M
- 🏷️ Total Products: 314
- ⚠️ Below Reorder Point: 28 products

🔍 Insight:
- Laptops have the lowest profit margin at 35% despite being the most expensive category.
- 28 products are dangerously close to running out of stock.

✅ Recommendation:
Review laptop pricing strategy and activate automated alerts
for any product reaching its Reorder Point.

4️⃣ Returns 



<img width="905" height="531" alt="1000097665" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f0cb29cd-9896-46f4-b5b9-03b41b7d4948" />



Key Numbers:
- ↩️ Total Returns: 461
- 📉 Return Rate: 19%
- 👨‍💼 Headcount: 173
- 🔄 Turnover Rate: 14%

🔍 Insight:
113 return cases were due to defective products — the leading return reason.
This points to a quality issue before products leave the warehouse,
not a logistics or customer preference problem.

✅ Recommendation:
Implement a mandatory Quality Check before every shipment,
along with a weekly report tracking the most returned products.

---

💡 Key Insights Summary

| Area | Insight | Recommendation |

| Sales | Revenue drops from July to October | Marketing campaigns in slow months |
| Customer | 19% Inactive customers | Targeted Retention campaign |
| Inventory | 28 products below reorder point | Automated reorder alerts |
| Returns | 113 cases due to defective products | Quality Check before shipping |
